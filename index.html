02 / WHY SOCIAL MEDIA?

What does this mean for your business?

Social media has become an important channel for businesses to share their stories, showcase their products, and build relationships with customers.

Today’s audiences encounter a constant stream of content. Businesses need creative, informative, and relevant advertising that captures attention and communicates a clear message.

Fresh media gives your business opportunities to show the people behind the brand, communicate your value, and remain visible to your target audience.

Industry insight

Forbes contributor Jamie Gutfreund reported on 8 July 2024 that, citing GWI, the average person spent 30% more time consuming social media than watching TV in 2023. This is a reported comparison of time spent, not a guarantee of advertising performance.
